Privacy Policy

Where's the Flock. Last updated October 6, 2026.

This app shows a map of cameras and sensors that are documented on the phone. It does not track you, and it does not show ads.

Location

The app uses your location on the phone to place the map and to show a nearby-camera alert. Location stays on the phone. It is not uploaded.

Reports

A report you make stays on the phone. It is not sent to us, to OpenStreetMap, or to any other list. The report can include the place, a chosen handle, and, when the equipment is recognizable, a function or manufacturer.

Photos, faces, and plates

If you attach a photo of the camera equipment, the photo is discarded on the phone after you submit. Faces and license plates are never kept.

Purchases

Plus is sold by Apple. Payment, renewal, and cancellation are handled by Apple with your Apple ID. We do not receive your card number.

Tracking and ads

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not show ads. Location is not used for ads and is not sold.
