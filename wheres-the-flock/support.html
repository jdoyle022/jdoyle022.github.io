What the map shows

The map shows cameras and sensors documented on this phone. Green pins are verified official records. Blue pins are confirmed on this phone. Orange pins are sightings. Purple pins are pending reports saved on this phone. A pin is a documented record. It does not mean a camera recorded you, and the app does not say that any camera is illegal.

Report camera

Report camera saves a pin on this phone. The report is not sent to a public list. A photo of the equipment is discarded on the phone. Faces and license plates are not kept.

Plus

The map is free. Plus is $2.99 a month, with a 14-day free trial, or $34.99 a year. Both plans auto-renew until you cancel. Cancel in Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Plus adds a nearby-camera alert and a second route drawn around known cameras already on the map. The cameras stay on the map. The route is not a promise that a camera will miss you.
